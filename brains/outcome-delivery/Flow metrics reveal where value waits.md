Flow metrics measure how work moves through a value stream. Measures such as flow time, velocity, efficiency, and load expose queues, overload, and bottlenecks that activity counts hide.

They locate constraints in delivery. They do not show whether the delivered work produced a valuable result; that requires [[Outcome metrics show whether delivery created value]].

[[Vanguard found its constraint before development]] is an example of measuring the whole path rather than the coding stage alone.

- Mik Kersten, *Output to Outcome: An Operating Model for the Age of AI*, 2026, chapter 5.
