> "The process of optimizing the Outcome Loop is the process of applying the Theory of Constraints to your planning and delivery processes."
>
> Mik Kersten, *Output to Outcome*

The Outcome Loop must improve how outcomes are delivered as well as measure whether they were delivered. Teams inspect the loop, find the constraint limiting flow, and change the planning or delivery system to remove it.

Flow improvement is therefore part of the loop itself. It is not maintenance deferred until after the outcome work.

[[Improving daily work is more important than doing daily work]] states the same principle at the level of everyday work. [[From function to flow]] describes the value-stream structure in which the loop operates.
