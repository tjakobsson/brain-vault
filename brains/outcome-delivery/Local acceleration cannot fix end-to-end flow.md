Making one stage faster barely changes total delivery time when most delay sits elsewhere. The system improves only when work on a local stage relieves the constraint on end-to-end flow.

In [[Vanguard found its constraint before development]], eliminating the entire 20-day development stage would reduce a 190-day path by only about 11 percent. The larger opportunity was the 120-day ideation stage.

This is why [[Improving flow belongs inside the Outcome Loop]] applies the Theory of Constraints to the whole planning and delivery system.
