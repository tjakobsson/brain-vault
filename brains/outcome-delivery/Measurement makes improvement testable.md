A change can improve a system without measurement, but the team cannot reliably know that it did. A baseline and a repeated measure turn improvement into a test: change the system, observe the result, and compare it with what came before.

Measurement does not decide what is valuable. [[Outcome metrics show whether delivery created value]] and [[Flow metrics reveal where value waits]] answer different parts of that question.

Prerequisite: [[Work must be visible before it can be measured]].
