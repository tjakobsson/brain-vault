Vanguard measured about 190 days from idea to production:

- Ideation took 120 days.
- Development took 20 days.
- Release, testing, and validation took 50 days.

The largest constraint was upstream of development. Measuring only coding would have hidden most of the delay and directed improvement toward the smallest stage.

This case illustrates why [[Flow metrics reveal where value waits]] and why [[Local acceleration cannot fix end-to-end flow]].
