Outcome metrics measure a change for customers, employees, or the business. They test whether delivered outputs had the intended effect instead of treating completed work as proof of value.

[[Flow metrics reveal where value waits]] describes how efficiently work moves. Outcome metrics describe whether that movement was worthwhile. The [[Outcome Management directs Agentic Engineering|Outcome Loop]] needs both.

## Sources

- Mik Kersten, *Output to Outcome: An Operating Model for the Age of AI*, 2026, chapters 1, 2, and 5.
