This brain follows the evidence needed to understand software composition, judge vulnerability exposure, and act on the result.

## Evidence chain

1. [[Software Bill of Materials]] records what a product contains.
2. [[Vulnerability Exploitability Exchange]] records whether a known vulnerability affects that product.
3. [[BOMHort]] joins inventories, vulnerability data, VEX statements, and policy into an operational system.
